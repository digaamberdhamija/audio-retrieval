Audio retrieval · Working prototype

Effective retrieval from audio transcripts

Ask a question in plain language and land on the exact moment in a two-speaker recording that answers it. Every response on this page was captured from the running service.

Why did NVIDIA choose video games as its first market? → AI podcast, 4:02 to 5:44

The approach

Index once, then search three window lengths at a time

Conversations range from rapid back-and-forth to twenty-minute monologues, so recordings are sliced by time rather than by speaker turn.

Per recording

  1. TranscribeTwo speakers and the time of every word
  2. Slice30, 60 and 120 s windows, each overlapping the next by half
  3. Embed768 numbers describing each window's meaning
  4. StorePostgres with a keyword index and a vector index

Per question

  1. Hybrid searchKeyword 60% and meaning 40%, at every window length
  2. JoinOverlapping matches become one passage
  3. RerankA local ColBERT model scores passages from all lengths together
  4. AnswerRepeats dropped; distinct moments with timestamps
30 s · 60 s · 120 s

Several window lengths

Long windows add noise around the answer, and short ones can cut an idea in half. Indexing all three gives every question a window that fits.

keyword 60 / meaning 40

Tilted towards exact words

Names, jargon and numbers win through keyword search, while meaning search catches questions phrased differently from how the speakers said it.

GTE-ModernColBERT-v1

One moment, shown once

A ColBERT reranker compares the question with each passage word by word, so a 30-second answer and a two-minute passage compete fairly.
